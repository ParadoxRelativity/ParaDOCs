Fonts bundled for slides (slideFonts.css).

Slides lean on Office fonts, so text must wrap where the author saw it. Each family here is metric-compatible with one:

- Carlito (SIL OFL 1.1) draws Calibri's space.
- Caladea (SIL OFL 1.1) draws Cambria's.
- Arimo, Tinos and Cousine (Apache 2.0) are the metric-compatible faces Liberation Sans, Serif and Mono 2.x are built from. They are registered under the Liberation names, for Arial, Times New Roman and Courier New.
- Inter (SIL OFL 1.1) is the default theme font.

Latin subset, regular and bold, upright and italic, from the @fontsource packages. The map from Office font names is mapOfficeFont in packages/shared/src/slideFonts.ts.
